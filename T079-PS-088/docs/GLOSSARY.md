
# GLOSSARY

1. SaaS: Software as a Service.
2. Subscription: A recurring agreement between a customer and company.
3. Plan: A package offered by the SaaS company.
4. Seat: One user license in a subscription.
5. Proration: Calculation of charges for the unused or remaining portion of a billing period.
6. Invoice: A document containing the amount payable by a customer.
7. GST: Goods and Services Tax.
8. CGST: Central Goods and Services Tax.
9. SGST: State Goods and Services Tax.
10. IGST: Integrated Goods and Services Tax.
11. Coupon: A discount applied to a subscription.
12. Account Credit: Money available in a customer's account to reduce future invoices.
13. Trial: A free subscription period.
14. Dunning: Process of retrying failed payments.
15. PAST_DUE: Subscription state after repeated payment failures.
16. UNPAID: Subscription state after the dunning failure threshold.
17. ACTIVE: Subscription is currently active.
18. SUSPENDED: Subscription access is suspended.
19. CANCELLED: Subscription has been terminated.
20. Renewal: Beginning of a new billing period.
21. Credit Note: Document recording a refund or reduction against an invoice.
22. Financial Year: Accounting year used for invoice numbering.
23. Billing Cycle: Period between two subscription renewals.
24. FIFO: First In, First Out.
